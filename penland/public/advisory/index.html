Penland Advisory

Outsourced bookkeeping, financial reporting, and operations consulting for restaurants, small businesses, and nonprofit organizations — plus the web development and project work those same clients need.

Services

  • Outsourced bookkeeping — full-cycle bookkeeping for restaurants and small businesses: daily sales reconciliation, accounts payable and receivable, payroll journal entries, bank and credit card reconciliation, and month-end close.
  • Tax filings and compliance — sales tax, meals tax, and related recurring filings with state and local authorities.
  • Financial reporting — monthly and quarterly financial packages for owners, partners, and investors.
  • Operations consulting — process design, systems selection, and reporting for owner-operated businesses and nonprofit organizations.
  • Web development and technology — websites, internal tools, and integrations built for the same clients we keep books for.
  • Event and program management — logistics, budgeting, and execution support on a statement-of-work basis.

Who we work with

Our clients are independent restaurants, owner-operated small businesses, and nonprofit organizations. Engagements run on a monthly retainer or a defined statement of work, and scope is reviewed each season rather than left to drift.

Penland Advisory is a bookkeeping and consulting practice. It does not provide audit, attest, legal, or investment advisory services, and it does not prepare or sign tax returns on behalf of clients.
