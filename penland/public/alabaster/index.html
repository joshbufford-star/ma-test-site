Alabaster Travel

Independent travel advisory — itinerary design, booking, and concierge support for leisure and group travel.

Travel advisory

Alabaster Travel plans and books travel for individuals, families, and groups — with a real person who knows the trip, from first idea through the flight home.

  • Itinerary design — destination research, pacing, and day-by-day planning built around what the travelers actually want.
  • Booking and coordination — accommodations, transportation, tours, and reservations, held together in one place.
  • Group travel — retreats, multi-family trips, and organized group departures, including rooming lists and payment tracking.
  • Concierge support — someone to call when a flight cancels or a plan changes mid-trip.

About the division

Alabaster Travel operates as a registered trade name of Penland LLC. Travel is booked through Alabaster Travel's host agency and supplier relationships; specific supplier terms, cancellation policies, and any applicable seller-of-travel disclosures are provided with each quote before payment is taken.
