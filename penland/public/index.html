Virginia Limited Liability Company

One company. Several lines of work.

Penland LLC owns and operates a small portfolio of businesses in property management, bookkeeping and advisory services, travel advisory, and software. Each division operates under its own name; all of them are the same legal entity, with the same books, the same insurance, and the same person answering the phone.

How the company is organized

Penland LLC is a single Virginia limited liability company. The divisions above are registered trade names, not separate companies. That means one federal Employer Identification Number, one set of books, one tax return, and one point of accountability for every client, tenant, and user.

Penland LLC also holds a passive equity investment in a restaurant operating in Richmond, Virginia. That holding is an investment of record only; it is not a customer-facing line of business and it is not offered to the public.
