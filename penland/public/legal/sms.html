1. Programs we operate

ProgramOperated byWhat it sends

2. How you opt in

You will receive text messages from a Penland program only if you affirmatively consent in one of these ways:

  • In the application. Checking the SMS consent checkbox in Match Lineup when you join a roster or edit your notification settings. The box is unchecked by default and appears separately from any other agreement.
  • On a web form. Checking an unchecked SMS consent box on a Penland web form that displays the program name, message frequency, rate disclosure, and opt-out instructions, together with links to these terms and our Privacy Policy.
  • By text. Texting a published keyword to our program number.

The consent language you agree to reads:

I agree to receive recurring automated SMS messages from Match Lineup (Penland LLC) about my team's lineups, schedule changes, and match assignments at the mobile number I provided. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or of participating on any team. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. See our SMS Terms and Privacy Policy.

Consent is never a condition of purchase and is never required to use any Penland service, to lease a property, to receive bookkeeping services, or to participate on a team. We never obtain consent from a third-party list, a lead vendor, or another company, and we never share the consent you give us with any other program or brand.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies. For Match Lineup Team Notifications, participants typically receive between 2 and 10 messages per month, depending on their team's season and how often their coach posts. For Penland Service Notifications, messages are sent only as service events occur.

4. Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Penland does not charge you for text messages, but your mobile carrier may charge you for sending or receiving them under your plan. Contact your carrier for details.

5. How to opt out — STOP

You can cancel at any time. Reply STOP to any message from the program. You may also text END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, or QUIT.

After you send one of these, we will send a single confirmation message and then stop:

"Match Lineup: You are unsubscribed and will receive no further messages. Reply START to rejoin or email support@penlandllc.com for help."

You may also turn notifications off in your Match Lineup settings or email support@penlandllc.com. To rejoin later, text START to the program number or re-enable notifications in your settings.

6. How to get help — HELP

Reply HELP to any message, or INFO. We will respond with:

"Match Lineup (Penland LLC) team notifications. Msg freq varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel. Help: support@penlandllc.com or [[BUSINESS_PHONE]]. Terms: penlandllc.com/legal/sms.html"
