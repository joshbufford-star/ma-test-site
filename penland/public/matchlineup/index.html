Match Lineup

Roster and match scheduling for competitive team programs. Coaches build lineups, publish match assignments, and keep athletes and parents informed — including by text message, when they have asked to be.

What Match Lineup does

Match Lineup is a web and mobile application operated by Penland LLC. A coach or program administrator creates a team, invites athletes and their parents or guardians, and uses the application to:

  • Maintain a team roster with contact information supplied by each participant.
  • Build and publish match lineups and individual match assignments.
  • Post schedule changes, venue changes, and call times.
  • Send those notifications to participants who have opted in to receive them.

Match Lineup is used by the teams that adopt it. It is not a marketing platform, it does not sell advertising, and it does not send messages to anyone who has not been added to a roster by their own coach and given consent.

Text message notifications

Match Lineup can deliver time-sensitive team notifications by SMS. This is an optional feature. It is off until the recipient turns it on.

How consent is collected

Consent is collected inside the Match Lineup application, at the moment a participant joins a team roster and again any time they edit their notification settings. No phone number receives messages until the box below has been checked by the person who owns that number (or by the parent or guardian of a minor participant).

Text me team notifications. I agree to receive recurring automated SMS messages from Match Lineup (Penland LLC) about my team's lineups, schedule changes, and match assignments at the mobile number I provided. Consent is not a condition of using Match Lineup or of participating on any team. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. See our SMS Terms and Privacy Policy.

The checkbox is unchecked by default and is presented separately from any other agreement. A participant can use every other part of Match Lineup without ever checking it.
